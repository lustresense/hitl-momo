# Sketchbook Universe: music, stingers and ambience (design + QA)

Status: generated by `tools/audio-gen` (`music/*.py`, `common/instr_music.py`). Original synthesis only (numpy/scipy). No samples, no downloads,
no AI audio model, no voices. Momo has no voice. **Not listened to by a human**: see "Honest disclaimer" at the end.

## 1. Musical identity: the Kite motif

C major pentatonic (C D E G A, which is also A minor pentatonic, so major and minor scenes share notes). Two bars of 4/4, lilting:

| bar | G4 (1 beat) | A4 (1/2) | C5 (1 1/2) | A4 (1) |   |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | G4:1 | A4:.5 | C5:1.5 | A4:1 | |
| 2 | G4:1 | E4:1 | D4:1 | rest:1 | (answer variant lands on C4) |

Transformations: dream = stretched x4 (one note per bar); cover = felt-piano top line over rolling arpeggios; tutorial = staccato marimba;
levels = call (bar x) / response (bar x+1) in celesta with echo; story = in parallel thirds (low felt piano) + celesta; focus = only single G/A/D plucks,
far apart, no line; l1 = bright marimba melody (octave up); l2 = transposed to G pentatonic (D E G E D B A) and syncopated; l3 = inverted (G E D E G A C) and half speed,
then the original at half speed lower; ending = augmentation (x2) that first ends on D (question) and then lands on C; jingles quote G-A-C.

## 3. Method

* Instruments (`common/instr_music.py`): felt piano (inharmonic additive partials, two-string beating, soft hammer, damper release), kalimba (sine body + beam-mode tines 1:6.27:17.5 + thumb click),
  marimba (bar partials 1:3.99:9.88 + mallet knock), pizzicato (additive plucked string, pluck-position comb, harmonics 2-4 boosted below 170 Hz for small speakers),
  pad (band-limited wavetable voices, 3 detunes, drift; `string` = triangle blooming to saw, `felt` = near-sine), bell/celesta (modal), shaker/brush/wood/tom/tick (filtered noise / modal).
  Every note is rendered once per (instrument, pitch, length, velocity bucket) with an RNG derived from that key.
* Sequencer: `Score` (mini notation `G4:1 A4:.5 [C4,E4]:2 r:1@0.8`, arpeggio patterns indexing a voicing, percussion grids), `Part` (gain, pan, HP/LP, reverb send, echo, chorus, swing).
  Humanisation from the asset RNG: timing +-5-10 ms (pads 0), velocity +-8-12 %. Composed patterns only, no random notes.
* Mix: each part is filtered (HP >= 70 Hz, LP 1.5-6 kHz), summed, a warm convolution reverb (own IR: three bands, highs decay faster, 1.6-4 s RT60) is added from per-part sends, master HP 70 Hz / LP 9.5 kHz, -1.5 dB at 3.3 kHz.
  Raw peak normalised to 0.2 so `finalize_loop` compression is gentle.
* Loops: render `bars` + 7 s tail, then `dsp.loop_wrap` folds notes and reverb ringing past the end onto the start; notes with negative jitter at the start wrap to the end. All notes start from silence, so the periodic signal is continuous.
  Ambience is built circularly (random-phase spectrum, periodic modulators), so it loops exactly.
* Mastering stays in `build_all.py`; `rms_db` per track was set so the integrated loudness lands near -23 LUFS (focus/dream -24.5).
* Determinism: same script gives identical decoded PCM (verified); the Ogg container serial differs per run, so file hashes change even though samples do not.
* Pitch sets are checked per track (`music/_check.py`): all pitched notes belong to the key set. Documented exceptions: F# in dream (Lydian #11), F# in l2 (D chord) and l3 (dorian 6th), F natural in l3 (Fmaj7).
  Known transient overlaps (crossfades of a departing pad note against a new melody note, >= 13 semitones apart or ringing out): dream beat 40, ending beat 56, l3 beat 32.

## 5. Honest disclaimer and known risks

* **Nobody has listened to this audio.** Everything above is measured (pitch accuracy of every instrument, spectra, spectrograms inspected as images, loop seams, loudness) and reasoned from standard recipes. Taste, balance and "does it feel like a kite in a sketchbook" are unverified.
* Risks: kalimba/marimba/pizzicato are physical-model-style approximations and may sound synthetic or "MIDI-like"; the convolution reverb is noise based and may sound smooth rather than natural;
  percussion levels were set by numbers (shakers sit 30-36 dB under the mix) and may be inaudible on laptop speakers; pads rely on detuned voices and chorus and may sound thin or wobbly;
  `bgm.focus` plucks (about 15 dB under the pad) may be too faint or still noticeable; the ticking in `bgm.l3` may feel tense to some children; `jingle.fail_soft` is major and consonant by design but untested for tone.
* Loops are 40-64 s with 16-24 bar A/B structures; fatigue over 5+ minutes is unproven. Loop seam is clean in the samples; browser looping gaps depend on the decoder.
* `dsp.fade(..., curve="cos")` has an inverted fade-out curve (it rises 0 to 1 at the end); the music code does not use it. `finalize_sfx`/stingers use the linear curve and are unaffected.
